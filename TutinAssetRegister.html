The estimated value at end of useful life, used for accounting depreciation only. Enter R1 if there is no meaningful resale value — this simply means the asset depreciates to the R1 minimum floor. Values above R1 reduce the depreciable base (e.g. a vehicle with R50,000 trade-in value). Tax depreciation always uses the full cost as the base and ignores this field entirely.
SARS depreciation method for the income tax return. S11(e) is the standard wear-and-tear allowance (most assets). S12C is the accelerated 40/20/20/20 allowance for qualifying manufacturing plant. Tax: None for assets that depreciate for accounting but carry no SARS tax depreciation. No Depreciation for assets that do not depreciate at all — neither for accounting nor for tax (e.g. bare land, fixed property held at cost with no write-off).
